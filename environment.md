# Environment used for the analyses

- OS: Windows 10 (results are platform-independent)
- Python: 3.12.13
  - pandas, numpy, statsmodels, lifelines
- R: 4.6.1 (figures only; not included in this repository)
  - survival, survminer, ggplot2

Data release: cBioPortal `msk_impact_50k_2026` (MSK-IMPACT clinical sequencing cohort)
and `brca_aurora_2023` (AURORA US Network), downloaded 2026-10/11.
